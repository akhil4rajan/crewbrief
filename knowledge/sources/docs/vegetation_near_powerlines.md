# Vegetation management near powerlines

## Planning

* Identify the voltage of every conductor near the trees and the applicable minimum approach distance.
* Where vegetation must be cut within the approach distance, arrange for the line to be isolated and
  earthed, or use authorised specialist vegetation crews with insulated tools.
* Check for wildlife, wasp nests and hollow or dead limbs before climbing or cutting.

## Cutting

* Never allow a branch to fall onto or across a live conductor: cut in small sections and control the
  fall with ropes where needed.
* Use insulated pole saws and pruners that are in test date for work near conductors.
* Chainsaw operators wear chaps, helmet with visor and hearing protection, and hold the relevant
  chainsaw competency.
* Set out an exclusion zone at least twice the height of the work and keep the public out of it.

## Trees in contact with lines

Treat any tree in contact with a conductor as live. Keep everyone clear and request isolation before
touching the tree or the ground around it.
