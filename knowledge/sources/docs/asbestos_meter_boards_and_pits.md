# Asbestos in meter boards and pits

Meter panels installed before about 1990 (including "zelemite" boards), asbestos-cement pits, pipes and
ducts can contain asbestos. Fibres are released when the material is drilled, cut, broken or
sanded.

## Before work

* Check the asbestos register and any labels. If the material is not confirmed asbestos-free, assume
  it contains asbestos.
* Plan the task so the board is not disturbed: reuse existing holes, mount new equipment on a separate
  panel, and do not drill, cut or snap the board.

## If minor disturbance is unavoidable

* Only workers trained in asbestos awareness and the approved minor-work procedure may proceed.
* Keep the material wet (spray bottle with PVA solution), use hand tools only, and capture debris with a
  HEPA vacuum. Never dry sweep or use compressed air.
* Wear a fit-checked P2 respirator and disposable coveralls.
* Bag and label waste as asbestos and dispose of it through the approved waste stream.

## Damaged boards

If a board is broken or crumbling, stop work, keep people clear, and report it so a licensed removalist
can make it safe.
