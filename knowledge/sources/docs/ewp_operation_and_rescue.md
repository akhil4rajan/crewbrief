# Elevated work platform operation and rescue

Applies to insulated and non-insulated boom-type EWPs (bucket trucks) used for pole-top, service and
streetlight work. Operators must hold the relevant high risk work licence (boom 11 m or more) and be
assessed on the specific unit.

## Pre-start

* Complete the logbook pre-start inspection: hydraulics, structure, tyres, outriggers, basket, harness
  anchor points, dielectric test date (insulated units) and the emergency lowering system.
* Function-test all controls from the basket and from the ground controls, including emergency stop
  and auxiliary lowering.
* Do not use a unit with an overdue major inspection or a defect tag.

## Set-up

* Assess the ground: avoid soft verges, recently backfilled trenches, pit lids and kerbs. Use outrigger
  pads or sole boards on every outrigger and level the truck within the manufacturer's limit.
* Position the truck to minimise reach and keep the boom clear of overhead conductors not being worked
  on. Where the boom could come within the approach distance of live lines, a spotter is required.
* Set out an exclusion (drop) zone under the basket with cones and bunting. On roads, the exclusion
  zone sits inside the traffic management set-up.

## Working aloft

* Wear a full-body harness attached to the basket anchor point at all times.
* Tether tools and use bucket bags so nothing falls into the drop zone.
* Stop work and lower the basket when wind exceeds 12.5 m/s (45 km/h) or the manufacturer's limit,
  or when lightning is within 10 km.

## EWP rescue

* A trained ground person must be present for the whole time the basket is raised and must know how
  to use the ground controls and auxiliary lowering.
* Talk through the rescue plan at the briefing: who performs the rescue, where the controls are, how
  to call 000, and the muster point for the ambulance.
* If the operator is incapacitated, lower the basket from the ground controls. If the operator is in
  contact with a live conductor, do not touch the truck: isolate the supply first.
