# Excavation and underground services

## Before you dig

* Obtain current Before You Dig Australia (BYDA) plans for every asset owner in the area. Plans are valid
  for the period stated on them; check they are on site and current.
* Plans show approximate locations only. Electronically locate all services (cable locator, GPR) and
  mark them out on the ground before breaking ground.
* Within the tolerance zone of a located service (typically 300 mm either side for electrical cables),
  expose the service by non-destructive digging: vacuum excavation (hydro-vac) or hand digging with
  insulated tools. Do not use mechanical excavators or pointed bars until the service is visible.
* If an unknown cable is found, treat it as live until identified, tested and isolated.

## Cable strike

If a cable is struck: stop, keep everyone clear of the excavation and plant, do not touch the plant if
it is in contact with the cable, call the control room to isolate, and preserve the scene.

## Trenches and open excavations

* Trenches deeper than 1.5 m must be shored, benched or battered, or a trench shield used.
* Keep spoil and plant at least 1 m back from the edge.
* Barricade open excavations with rigid barriers or fencing and plate or cover them when unattended.
  Bunting alone is not sufficient where the public has access.
* Provide safe access and egress (ladder) for excavations deeper than 1.5 m.

## Pits

Cable pits and asbestos-cement pits: see the confined spaces and asbestos documents before opening or
entering a pit.
